About HiganveinOS

Born from solitude, dark fantasy aesthetics, and blistering performance.

The Origin of Higanvein

HiganveinOS is forged for those who prefer quiet isolation, dark rooms bathed in minimal ambient light, and a system that operates with absolute precision without unnecessary noise.

Taking inspiration from the quiet beauty of Red Spider Lilies (Higanbana) and deep psychological anime narratives, HiganveinOS is built as a sanctuary away from social noise. It's a high-performance Arch-based operating system designed to give developers, gamers, and ricing enthusiasts total control over their hardware.

Built by Passion

Created independently as a passion project, HiganveinOS stands on the shoulders of giants—utilizing the rock-solid foundation of Arch Linux and the raw optimizations of the CachyOS kernel. We believe your operating system should reflect your inner focus: fast, silent, and flawlessly riced.
