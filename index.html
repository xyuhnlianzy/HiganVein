Engineering Principles

Built Different by Design

We reject telemetry, hidden analytics, and manufactured bloat. Every layer is inspectable and reproducible.

CachyOS BORE Scheduler

Burst-Oriented Response Enhancer kernel balances interactive task latency without starving compilation or background rendering threads.

Proactive Memory Defense

Automatic ZRAM swap (2.5× physical RAM via zstd) combined with tuned earlyoom kill policies. Eliminates hard UI freezes under heavy memory pressure.

Btrfs Instant Rollback

Standard subvolume topology (@, @home, @snapshots) integrated with Snapper GUI and GRUB snapshots for instant single-reboot disaster recovery.

Independent Attribution & Zero Telemetry Guarantee

HiganveinOS is engineered independently by D-P. We collect zero analytics, harvest zero telemetries, and place zero tracking cookies on your device. What runs on your machine stays entirely on your machine.
